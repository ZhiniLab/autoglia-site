## Initial Findings

### What Makes Founders Successful (from Paul Graham + research)

1. **Make something people want** - Core principle
2. **Start with good people** - Team matters
3. **Spend as little as possible** - Bootstrapping mindset
4. **Better to make a few people really happy than a lot of people semi-happy** - Focus
5. **Formidable founders + promising market + evidence of prior success** - The trifecta

---

## Marketing Patterns of Successful Founders

### Key Observations from Research:

1. **Building in Public**
   - Share progress, failures, learnings
   - Twitter/X is primary channel for tech founders
   - Authenticity over polish

2. **Content Types That Work**
   - Thread format (12-tweet threads work well)
   - Single punchy insights
   - Pain point identification + solution offering
   - Behind-the-scenes development updates

3. **Posting Frequency**
   - Most successful founders post daily
   - Consistency > viral
   - Mix of: insights, questions, updates, engagement

4. **Tone**
   - Direct, opinionated
   - Helpful, not salesy
   - Admitting mistakes/problems
   - Specific examples over generic advice

5. **Community Engagement**
   - Reply to others in niche
   - Retweet with added insight
   - Build relationships with peers
