Privacy Policy

Autoglia is designed to keep your memory data on your machine. This policy describes what autoglia.com and related services may process.

What we do not collect from the software

  • We do not host your SQLite database (memory.db) from the Autoglia skill.
  • The skill does not phone home or send telemetry.

Website and GitHub

  • Visiting autoglia.com is subject to normal web hosting and DNS processing.
  • Using GitHub (repository, Issues, Discussions) is subject to GitHub’s privacy policy.

Email support

If you email support@autoglia.com, we process your message and address to respond. We do not sell email addresses.

Legacy purchase records

If you previously purchased a product under prior terms, limited records may have been kept for support and delivery. You may request removal of support correspondence by contacting the same email.

Contact

Privacy questions: support@autoglia.com.
