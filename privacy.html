Privacy Policy

Autoglia is built to keep customer data local and minimize collection. This page covers what data we process for purchases, update delivery, and support.

What We Collect

  • Purchase email address for product delivery and update eligibility.
  • Download token metadata (token id, expiry, download count, product version).
  • Support emails you send us voluntarily.

What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not host your memory database contents from Autoglia itself.
  • We do not run analytics trackers on product memory data.
  • We do not sell customer data.

How Delivery Works

After purchase, a time-limited download token is generated and emailed. The token is used only to authorize file delivery and enforce download limits.

Retention

Purchase and token records are retained for customer support, update access, and abuse prevention. You can request account-level delivery record removal by emailing support@autoglia.com.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@autoglia.com.
