## What We Know About Them

### HEAV (Home Educators Association of Virginia)
- **Founded:** Virginia grassroots organization
- **Members:** Used by ~20,000 parents
- **They offer:** Their own transcript service ($9.95-$24.95/month)
  - Standard: $9.95/mo — transcript, auto-suggest, GPA calc
  - Premium: $17.95/mo + AUDIT (error checking)
  - Expert Review: $24.95/mo + expert comments
- **Strengths:** Virginia-specific, mentorship program, conferences, legislative advocacy

### HSLDA (Home School Legal Defense Association)
- **Founded:** Christian organization, 501(c)(3)
- **Members:** 90,000+ families nationwide
- **Strengths:** Legal defense, legislative advocacy, large reach
