## What Makes Founders Successful (Summary)

### Core Principles (Paul Graham + Research)
1. **Make something people want** - The #1 rule
2. **Start with good people** - Team quality matters
3. **Spend as little as possible** - Bootstrapping wins
4. **Few people very happy > many people semi-happy** - Focus
5. **The trifecta**: formidable founders + promising market + prior success

---

## Marketing Patterns of Successful Founders

### What Works on X/Twitter:
- **Building in public** - Share progress, failures, learnings
- **Daily posting** - Consistency over viral
- **Thread format** - 12-tweet threads work great
- **Pain point content** - Identify problems + offer solutions
- **Authenticity** - Admit mistakes, show behind-the-scenes

### Tone:
- Direct and opinionated
- Helpful, not salesy  
- Specific examples > generic advice
- Question-asking engages

### Content Mix:
- 40% Educational/insights
- 30% Community engagement
- 20% Personal/progress updates
- 10% Product/promotional
