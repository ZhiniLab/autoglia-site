**1/12** I asked 100 AI developers what their #1 pain point is.

PAIN. POINT.

**2/12** Memory.

Not "how do I fit more in the context window."

Actual persistent memory that survives between sessions.

The AI forgets EVERYTHING when you start a new conversation.

---

**3/12** Here's what happens now:

- You build something cool with AI
- End the session
- Start a new one
- It's like talking to a stranger who has no idea what you did before

Repeat. Every. Single. Time.

---

**4/12** Developers are coping in wild ways:

🔹 Copy-pasting previous conversations manually
🔹 Building custom memory systems from scratch
🔹 Just... accepting the limitation
🔹 Using cloud services (but then your data isn't private)

None of these are good solutions.

---

**5/12** The wild thing?

This is SOLVABLE. We've had databases for decades.

But AI developers are so busy building the AI part, they forget the memory part.

And the big AI companies? They kinda benefit from you not having memory (more tokens = more $)

---

**6/12** Some solutions exist:

- ByteRover: team/shared memory
- MemOS: cloud-based
- Context7: code-specific

But they're either:
- Team-focused (not personal)
- Cloud-based (privacy concerns)
- Code-specific (narrow use case)

---

**7/12** The gap: **personal memory for individual agents**

Your agent, remembering YOU, your preferences, your project.

Fully local. Your data stays on your machine.

That's what we've been building.
